# Hoys premium logo exploration

## Goal
Create a stronger identity for **Hoys (hoys.ai)** that feels credible at global, category-defining scale: elegant, memorable, and understated rather than glossy or generically “AI.” The reference point is Instinct’s confidence and restraint, not a copy of its lettering or symbol.

## Direction
Use a disciplined core palette:
- near-black / deep ink for authority
- warm ivory to connect with the current Hoys site
- vivid cobalt as the recognizable digital accent
- lime only as a rare supporting accent, never the main logo color

The existing cream/cobalt/lime identity remains compatible, but the logo itself becomes simpler and more premium.

## Four distinct concepts
1. **Bespoke lowercase** — custom “hoys” lettering with one ownable cut or ligature; quiet, editorial, and instantly legible.
2. **Horizon H** — a compact abstract H built from two precise planes, paired with restrained lettering; strongest for an app icon and favicon.
3. **Signal** — a minimal symbol suggesting coordinated work, flow, and intelligence without using a spark, robot, brain, or chat bubble.
4. **Institutional serif** — high-fashion/editorial lettering balanced with a sharp modern detail; the most elevated and unconventional option.

Each concept will be structurally different, not a color swap.

## Deliverables
- Four new premium PNG logo candidates, each saved separately
- Transparent backgrounds with generous margins
- Exact lowercase name: **hoys**
- No tagline, mockup, scene, or extra wording
- Checked at site-header size and on both light and dark surfaces
- A concise recommendation of the strongest candidate and why

The website will not be changed during this exploration. After you choose a candidate, it can be refined from that exact image and then applied across the site.

## Quality checks
Inspect every candidate for exact spelling, clean transparency, edge damage, spacing, clipping, small-size readability, and contrast. Correct concrete defects before presenting the set.
