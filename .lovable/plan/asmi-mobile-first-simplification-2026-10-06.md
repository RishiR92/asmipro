# Asmi Mobile-First Simplification

## Goal
Make the opening instantly understandable on a phone, then present founder and company credibility in a clean, aligned format that scales sharply to desktop.

## Changes

### 1. Simplify the opening
- Remove the “For plumbers, electricians, HVAC and home service pros” line in both languages.
- Keep **“Paid jobs. No lead fees.”** as the single dominant message.
- Replace the current subtitle and separate language line with one short supporting sentence that communicates both outcomes: customers pay first, and Asmi handles office work by text or voice.
- Keep one prominent waitlist button and reduce supporting checks to the minimum needed for confidence.
- Tighten the illustration and paid-job example so they support the message without competing with it.
- Rebuild spacing and type hierarchy around the selected **Sharp tech-modern mobile** direction, while retaining Asmi’s cream, cobalt, lime, and riso artwork.

### 2. Clean up trust and founder proof
- Reformat the trust strip into a compact, aligned proof block using recognizable Meta, Google, and Snap references.
- Change the backer message to **“Backed by leaders at Snap, Meta, Google.”** and remove Suno everywhere visible.
- Keep the supplied Rish and Satwik portraits in cleaner, consistently sized founder cards.
- Update the English founder copy exactly to:
  - **Rish:** “Built Asia's largest tech platform for small store owners: 100,000+ shops. Backed by top investors and founders of DoorDash and Zoom.”
  - **Satwik:** “Built AI systems at Meta (Facebook). PhD from Carnegie Mellon. Previously worked at Google.”
- Update the Spanish copy to match the same meaning.
- Remove the Humint Labs / Sunnyvale / “20,000+ real-world tasks” line from the founder section.
- Replace the current dense individual backer list with a simpler company-level credibility presentation, avoiding unfamiliar names and titles.

### 3. Remove unwanted sections and links
- Remove the FAQ section from the landing page in both languages.
- Remove Privacy and Terms links from the footer and waitlist form.
- Remove the standalone Privacy and Terms pages so those URLs no longer expose draft legal copy.
- Keep the required waitlist contact-consent checkbox, but make it a plain consent statement without legal-page links.

### 4. Responsive refinement
- Make the hero, trust proof, founder cards, and footer mobile-first at the current 394px viewport.
- Use a balanced two-column founder layout and restrained content width on desktop.
- Check that text wraps cleanly, cards align, and no element overflows at mobile and desktop widths.
